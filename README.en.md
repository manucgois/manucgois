## 🚀 Featured projects

### ✍️ Ratifica — Document generation and digital signature
Native Android app built as a team for the **FIAP Challenge with LetsSign** (a real-world industry challenge): users generate documents and sign them digitally from their phone.

- Built with **Kotlin**, **Jetpack Compose** and dependency injection with **Hilt**
- **Reusable signature profile**, created on first login (name, role, professional ID and company)

🔗 [View repository](https://github.com/manucgois/letssign-mobile)

### 🌍 CarbonIQ — Environmental impact simulator
Native Android app focused on the **Environmental pillar of ESG**: users simulate the carbon footprint of their activities, save their history and track their running total.

- 5 screens (Home, New Simulation, Result, History and Tips) built with **Jetpack Compose** and Navigation
- Local persistence with **Room** + **ViewModel** architecture
- Integration with the public **Open-Meteo Air Quality** API via **Retrofit**

🔗 [View repository](https://github.com/manucgois/carboniq-android)

### ⚡ CarbonIQ Energy — Database automation with PL/SQL
An evolution of CarbonIQ to monitor **real energy consumption**. Relational modeling (ERD) and automations with **PL/SQL triggers** around *energy efficiency and sustainability*.

🔗 [View repository](https://github.com/manucgois/carboniq-energia)
